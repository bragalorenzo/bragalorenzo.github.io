Research profile

My research connects long-term vegetation records with questions about plant biodiversity and ecosystem change. I combine palaeopalynology, numerical ecology and biodiversity informatics to improve both the interpretation of fossil pollen records and the reuse of the data they generate.

Wetland palaeoecology

Teor-Modeano palaeochannel · INF1

A fossil pollen record from a palaeochannel peatland in Friuli Venezia Giulia, northeastern Italy, used to investigate long-term vegetation and wetland dynamics.

Malafede Pool · CPZ2

A fossil pollen record from a vernal pool in the Castelporziano estate, Latium, used to study past vegetation, land use and environmental change.

My analytical work includes diversity and compositional change, multivariate analysis, community trajectories and quantitative vegetation reconstruction. These approaches support the interpretation of pollen assemblages at the community and landscape scales.

PROTO · Pollen-flora Record Overlapping Tool

PROTO is a web application that connects pollen types with floristic records through taxonomic identifiers. It quantifies pollen–flora overlap and helps constrain the plant taxa that may be represented by a pollen assemblage.

The tool provides a bridge between pollen-level observations and species-level botanical information, supporting the ecological interpretation of fossil records.

BRAIN · Biodiversity data integration

Within the Botanical Records of Archaeobotany Italian Network (BRAIN), my work addresses the standardisation of palaeopalynological data using Darwin Core and their integration into the LifeWatch Italy Data Portal.

This work focuses on taxonomic reconciliation, data structure and reusability, so that palaeoecological records can be connected with broader biodiversity infrastructures.
